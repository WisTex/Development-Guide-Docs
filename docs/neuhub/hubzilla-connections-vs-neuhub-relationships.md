# Hubzilla Connections vs. Neuhub Relationships

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Hubzilla uses the term **Connection** for relationships between channels.

While technically accurate, the term "Connection" can describe several different types of relationships that users think about differently.

Neuhub may use more specific terminology in its user interface to better match user expectations while still using Hubzilla's underlying connection system.

<hr>

## Hubzilla's Model

In Hubzilla, relationships between channels are generally created through connections.

A connection can be used to:

* Follow public content
* Share private content
* Communicate with friends and family
* Participate in forums and communities
* Grant permissions to other channels

From a technical perspective, these relationships are all forms of connections.

<hr>

## Neuhub's Model

Neuhub may present different relationship types using terminology that better reflects how users think about them.

### Follow

Used primarily for public accounts that broadcast content to a wide audience.

Examples:

* News organizations
* Content creators
* Bloggers
* Businesses
* Public figures

Users typically think of these relationships as following rather than connecting.

In most cases, the user is primarily interested in receiving content from the account.

### Connect

Used primarily for personal relationships.

Examples:

* Friends
* Family members
* Coworkers
* Professional contacts

Users typically think of these relationships as connecting with another person.

Although Hubzilla does not require a reciprocal connection, private content generally becomes available only when both parties establish a relationship and grant appropriate permissions.

### Join

Used primarily for forums, communities, and discussion groups.

Examples:

* Discussion forums
* Interest groups
* Community spaces
* Support communities

Users typically think of these relationships as joining a community rather than connecting to an account.

<hr>

## Technical Notes

The terms Follow, Connect, and Join are user interface concepts.

Under the hood, Hubzilla continues to use its existing connection system.

These terms do not represent different protocols or different relationship mechanisms. They are alternative ways of presenting Hubzilla's capabilities in a manner that is easier for users to understand.

<hr>

## Scope

This document explains how Neuhub may present relationship terminology differently from standard Hubzilla terminology.

It does not alter Hubzilla's underlying connection model or permission system.
